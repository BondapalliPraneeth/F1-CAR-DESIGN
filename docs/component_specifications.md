## Aerodynamic components

### Front Wing <a id="front-wing"></a>

`id: front_wing` · category **aerodynamic** · panel **front_wing** · susceptibility **high**

- **Position:** Ahead of the front axle, mounted beneath the nose
- **Purpose:** Generates front-axle downforce and conditions the airflow for everything downstream.
- **Primary function:** Front downforce generation and wake management around the front tyres.
- **Construction:** Typically a multi-element carbon-fibre composite structure with endplates.
- **Performance contribution (model):** Model share: 26% of total downforce, 8% of total drag. Largest single lever on aerodynamic balance.
- **Susceptibility note:** Most exposed component on the car; first contact point in lap-one incidents.

The front wing operates in clean air and in ground effect. Losing an element reduces front load directly, moving the aerodynamic balance rearwards and causing understeer. Endplate damage mainly degrades outwash control around the front tyres, so tyre wake ends up in the floor inlet and the floor also loses load.

**Typical failure modes**

- Endplate detachment after wheel-to-wing contact
- Flap or element failure
- Pylon / mounting failure
- Flutter under load

### Floor <a id="floor"></a>

`id: floor` · category **aerodynamic** · panel **floor** · susceptibility **high**

- **Position:** Underside of the car, between the axles
- **Purpose:** Main ground-effect surface; seals the underfloor flow.
- **Primary function:** Underfloor downforce with low drag cost.
- **Construction:** Typically carbon-fibre composite with a sacrificial wear plank.
- **Performance contribution (model):** Model share: 20% of downforce, 4% of drag. Highly sensitive to ride height.

The floor accelerates air underneath the car, lowering pressure. The edges act as an aerodynamic seal. When an edge is damaged, high-pressure air leaks in and all underfloor devices (tunnels and diffuser) lose load.

**Typical failure modes**

### DRS Mechanism <a id="drs"></a>

`id: drs` · category **aerodynamic** · panel **rear_wing** · susceptibility **low**

- **Position:** Upper rear-wing flap and actuator
- **Purpose:** Drag reduction system: opens the upper flap on straights to cut drag.
- **Primary function:** Straight-line drag reduction.
- **Construction:** Typically a hydraulic actuator and a hinged carbon flap.
- **Performance contribution (model):** Model share: 3% of downforce, 3% of drag; opening removes a configurable fraction of rear-wing load and drag.

Opening the flap stalls the upper element so rear-wing drag and load fall together. If it fails open, the car loses rear downforce in corners. If it fails closed, the straight-line gain is lost.

### Front Brake Assembly <a id="front-brakes"></a>

`id: front_brakes` · category **thermal** · panel **brakes** · susceptibility **medium**

- **Position:** Inside the front wheel rims
- **Purpose:** Convert kinetic energy to heat to slow the car; front axle carries most brake torque.
- **Primary function:** Deceleration and braking stability.
- **Construction:** Typically carbon-carbon discs and pads with multi-piston calipers and cooling ducts.
